# CTA Card Resource

Seniors aged 65 and older can ride CTA buses at a reduced fare or for free through the RTA Reduced Fare Permit or the Illinois Benefit Access Program.

## Requirements

| Category | Requirement |
|:--|:--|
| **Age Requirement** | _65 years or older_ |
| **Residency** | _Must be an Illinois resident_ |
| **Medicare Cardholders** | _All seniors with a valid Medicare card are eligible to apply for a Reduced Fare Permit, even if under 65, if they do not have a disability._ |
| **Benefit&nbsp;Access&nbsp;Program** | _Seniors enrolled in the Illinois Department on Aging’s Benefit Access Program may qualify for free transit on CTA, Metra, and Pace fixed routes._ |
| **Reduced Fare Permit** | _The RTA Reduced Fare Permit allows seniors to ride CTA buses and trains at a reduced fare. <BR> The permit can store value to pay fares on CTA and Pace, and value can be added at CTA rail station vending machines._ |

- Maximum value on the card is $100.
- The permit is personal and cannot be shared with others. 

## Application Process

| Category | Detail |
|:--|:--|
| **Online Application** | _Apply at [fares.rtachicago.org](http://fares.rtachicago.org) to receive your permit within 7–10 business days._ | 
| **Paper Application** | _Download the form from the RTA website, include a 2” x 2” color photo, a copy of a valid photo ID (driver’s license, state ID, passport, or Chicago CityKey card), and mail it to the address on the application._ |
| **Medicare&nbsp;Verification** | _Medicare cardholders must provide proof of Medicare status, which can be obtained from the Social Security Administration._ |
| **Replacement Permits** | _Lost or damaged permits can be replaced for a fee ($5 for the first replacement, $10 for additional replacements)._ |

## Benefit Access Program (Free Transit)

- Seniors enrolled in the Benefit Access Program may receive free rides on CTA buses and trains.  
- Eligibility is determined by submitting the Benefit Access Application every other year and meeting income and residency requirements. 
- Once approved, seniors receive a certificate and must contact CTA to obtain the free ride benefit. 

## Using the Card
- Show your Reduced Fare Permit or Benefit Access certificate when boarding CTA buses or entering train stations.
Reduced Fare Permits can be used for transfers and to pay fares on CTA and Pace services. 
- Seniors in Chicago can take advantage of these programs to save on transit costs, with options for both reduced fare and free rides depending on eligibility and enrollment in state programs.
