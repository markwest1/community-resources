# South Chicago Community Resources

This summary covers resources compiled across multiple documents, organized by category. Many resources serve all of Chicago, with several locations specifically in or near the South Side.

---

## 🆘 Emergency Hotlines & Starting Points

**Call 311** (or 312-744-5000) for shelter placement, homeless prevention assistance, and referrals to food, clothing, and other services. TTY access available at (312) 948-6817.

**211 Metro Chicago** — Call or visit [211metrochicago.org](https://211metrochicago.org) for live assistance 24/7 with food, counseling, income support, employment, healthcare, and more. All calls are free, anonymous, and confidential.

**Illinois Respite Coalition** — For caregivers of people with disabilities, mental illness, Alzheimer's, or chronic conditions. Call 866-455-7377 (ext. 101 English, ext. 103 Spanish) for a free break/respite service.

## ⚡ Utility Assistance

### CEDA – Energy Services Department
Apply at [CEDAorg.net/Bills](https://www.cedaorg.net/Bills) or call (800) 571-2332.

- **LIHEAP (Low-Income Home Energy Assistance Program) – Direct Vendor Payment (DVP):** One-time benefit applied directly to heating or electric bills.
- **Reconnection Assistance (RA):** Up to $1,500 to prevent disconnection or reconnect heating/electric services.
- **Cash Assistance (CA):** For renters whose heating is included in rent.
- **Peoples Gas Share the Warmth:** Up to $200 toward a past-due Peoples Gas balance.
- **Energy Savings Kit (ESK):** Free kit of energy-saving products to lower monthly bills.
- **Utility Billing Relief (UBR):** 50% reduction on water, sewer, and tax rates, plus potential full debt forgiveness for City of Chicago residents.

**Income guidelines (30-day gross):** $2,510 (1 person) up to $8,427 (8 people).

### Low-Income Discount Rate (LIDR)
Monthly discount on natural gas bills in five income-based tiers (based on Federal Poverty Level):

| Utility | Tier 1 (0–50% FPL) | Tier 2 (51–100%) | Tier 3 (101–150%) | Tier 4 (151–200%) | Tier 5 (201–300%) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Peoples Gas | 83% | 68% | 45% | 20% | 5% |
| North Shore Gas | 79% | 60% | 36% | 12% | 5% |
| Nicor Gas | 75% | 55% | 25% | 10% | 5% |

Complete a LIHEAP application at [CEDAorg.net/Bills](https://www.cedaorg.net/Bills) to qualify.

### Peoples Gas Medical Certificate
Customers with serious medical conditions may qualify for additional protections against gas service disconnection. Contact Peoples Gas directly for the Medical Certificate process.

## 🚌 Transportation

### CTA Senior Reduced Fare
Seniors 65+ (or Medicare cardholders) can ride CTA at reduced fare or for free through:
- **RTA Reduced Fare Permit** — Apply at [fares.rtachicago.org](http://fares.rtachicago.org) (7–10 business days) or by paper application with a 2"x2" photo and photo ID.
- **Illinois Benefit Access Program** — Seniors meeting income/residency requirements may ride CTA, Metra, and Pace fixed routes for free. Apply every other year.
- Maximum card value: $100. Permits are non-transferable.

### Medicaid Transportation
For medical appointments, Medicaid covers transportation through **First Transit**:
1. Call your transportation provider or First Transit at **1-877-725-0569** to arrange a trip.
2. Call First Transit to get the trip approved (have your Medicaid ID, appointment details, and pick-up address ready).
3. Schedule pick-up with the transportation company using your reference number.

## 🆔 IDs & Birth Certificates

Individuals experiencing homelessness (living in shelter, outdoors, or doubled-up) are eligible for **free birth certificates and state IDs**. Request a homeless verification letter from your case manager or STLS liaison to access this benefit.
